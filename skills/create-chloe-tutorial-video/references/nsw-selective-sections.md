# Choose the section before writing the lesson

Chloe is in Year 5 in 2026. Her 2027 Selective High School Placement Test is for entry to Year 7 in 2028. The Department lists 13 or 14 May 2027 as the main test dates. Each child attends one allocated day. Recheck the Department's current cohort on a later run before citing dates, counts or marking requirements. [Practice guidance and student video transcript](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/parents-and-carers/choosing-a-school-setting/selective-high-schools/placement-test/selective-high-school-practice-tests.html) · [Placement procedures](https://education.nsw.gov.au/policy-library/policies/pd-2002-0006-06)

| Section | Published format | Teaching decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading | 17 questions in 45 minutes. Three have multiple parts. The student guide counts 38 answers overall. | Keep the passage or extracts visible. Tie each inference to words in the text. A comparison or multipart item needs passage-specific instructions and an answer for each part. |
| Mathematical Reasoning | 35 multiple-choice questions in 40 minutes. Five possible answers. | Use a problem solvable without a calculator. Show units and how the quantities change. Check the key, the displayed letter and all five choices. |
| Thinking Skills | 40 multiple-choice questions in 40 minutes. Four possible answers. | Test what the supplied rules permit. Build a counterexample or enumerate valid cases. Show the given conditions and all four choices on an exam-format prediction. |
| Writing | One typed open response in 30 minutes. | Preserve the topic, instructions, reader and purpose. Let Chloe plan, draft, revise and try a fresh prompt. Show how a different valid approach could work. |

All four sections carry 25% weighting. The department describes ideas, purpose, audience, language choices and writing effectiveness. Verify official marking claims separately. Give specific feedback on what the words achieve for the reader. Any teaching scaffold, example opening or suggested planning time must be identified as an instructional choice. [Official section descriptions](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/parents-and-carers/choosing-a-school-setting/selective-high-schools/placement-test/selective-high-school-practice-tests.html)

Teach one manageable idea at a time in language a ten- or eleven-year-old can explain back. Label a shortened example as a guided exercise when it differs from the exam format. For an original exam-style task, match the relevant response form. A textbook topic or an original question needs evidence of a relevant tested skill. Describe it as original preparation material.
