# Independent content and transcript review

## Fix the scope

Identify the pupil, school stage, test year, entry year, section, teaching objective and exact file or player revision. For Chloe, the 2027 NSW selective high school test is for Year 7 entry in 2028. Consult the Department's dedicated placement and selective high school practice pages for that cohort whenever the lesson claims a date, question count, response form, rule or marking criterion. Record the URL, access date and relevant section. A supplied textbook can define the teaching sequence. Use the Department for exam facts. [Placement test](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/parents-and-carers/choosing-a-school-setting/selective-high-schools/placement-test) · [Official selective high school practice guide](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/parents-and-carers/choosing-a-school-setting/selective-high-schools/placement-test/selective-high-school-practice-tests.html)

Map each requested knowledge point to its lesson, question or timestamp and to a fresh application. Mark an absent point explicitly. Mark a clip-only review as partial. A few good samples can demonstrate capability; a complete course coverage statement needs an inventory of every required point and every delivered lesson.

## Check what the agent taught

Solve or respond to the learner-visible task before consulting the proposed key. Then check every explanation, choice, labelled diagram, model response and practice answer against the source or original givens. Inspect the learner's prediction and answer frames. Two matching copies of the same wrong statement still fail. Record the independent working and any ambiguity.

| Section | Decisive checks |
| --- | --- |
| Reading | Read the whole passage. For each claim, distinguish direct evidence, reasonable inference and unsupported detail. Verify quoted words against the original. Check each answer or part. |
| Mathematical Reasoning | Recompute from the original units and givens, preferably a second way or by substitution. Check the matching option letter, distractors and displayed objects. In the verified NSW format an exam-style item has five options and can be solved without a calculator. |
| Thinking Skills | For a necessity claim try to break it with a valid counterexample. For a possibility claim construct a complete case. Check rule direction, missing alternatives, the answer letter and all four options in an exam-style item. |
| Writing | Read the prompt before the model response. Check topic, required details, audience, purpose and form. Evaluate the response itself and whether the revision achieves the claimed effect. Consider another valid approach. Check grammar, punctuation and spelling in the final text. Flag invented compulsory structures, word counts and guaranteed marks. The exam uses a typed open response. |

Check that new vocabulary is explained in context, Australian English is used for the learner-facing text, and the necessary information is available to a Year 5 pupil. For material with fewer choices, a shortened time or a single technique, accurately label it as a teaching exercise. Preserve the original when the user supplies an official question. Attribute derivative teaching examples accurately.

## Check what Chloe actually hears and reads

1. After independent content checking, lock the **approved spoken script** for each beat. Retain intentional reading pauses. Document legitimate differences between spoken words and on-screen notation, such as saying “one quarter” for ¼ on a diagram.
2. Obtain the **released audio**, the visible captions or active subtitle track, and the learner-facing transcript. Decode the released media to measure duration and transcribe its speech independently. Record a hash or other media identity and the examined time range. A TTS input file or a regenerated subtitle draft cannot establish what was released.
3. Compare the entire spoken sequence, caption text and transcript with the approved script. Check sentence order and attached units. Tolerate harmless punctuation, case and confirmed written/spoken equivalents. Review differences involving a sign, denominator, decimal, time, unit, name, negation, quantifier, pronoun, quoted word, option label or omitted sentence. Read every caption on its own at its reveal time. Keep negatives and qualifications with the claims they govern, and amounts with their units. Check that each cue is legible, coincides with the spoken scene and preserves the prediction pause.
4. Resolve ambiguous speech by replaying the disputed phrase against the givens; another recogniser can supply additional evidence. Where the only ambiguity is phonetic or spelling, keep the reference words on screen and record why the interpretation is sound. Rephrase or re-record genuinely unclear teaching. Re-render and recheck the affected media, captions, chapters and transcript. Keep unresolved meaning **unverified**.
5. The optional standard-library checker reads a JSON evidence manifest and compares approved text, captions and independent readback. Follow its CLI help and evidence format. Supply the actual learner transcript body in publishedTranscript. A missing transcript has a separate unverified result. The checker flags differences for adjudication. Verify the learner-facing transcript independently and inspect the burned-in captions or the subtitle track used by the player. Record which cues were directly inspected. ## Record what passed

For each lesson record a separate **meets**, **revise** or **unverified** for content accuracy, requested coverage, exam alignment, spoken words, visible captions and transcript, and visual teaching. Include a source or timecode for each issue, a correction and evidence from the final revision. Report “all items checked” only when every expected item has evidence and all material discrepancies have been resolved. Describe any machine-only checks, incomplete audition or partial review. A complete check can still miss an error.
